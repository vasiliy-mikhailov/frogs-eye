Each request contributes prompt_tokens / TTFT and completion_tokens / (end − TTFT). Percentiles are over the sliding window. For a RATE the SLA floor is p05 (the 5th percentile) — 95% of requests were at least that fast. Written zero-padded so it cannot be misread as p95. p95 of a rate is the best-case tail, which is why p95 > p50 here and why it is not the number to promise. Non-streaming requests have no first-token event and are excluded from rate percentiles (counted under measurable).
Prefill rate includes queue wait, because TTFT is measured at the client edge and the proxy cannot see vLLM's internal queue time. On a saturated engine this understates true prefill speed — compare against vllm:request_queue_time_seconds to separate the two.
